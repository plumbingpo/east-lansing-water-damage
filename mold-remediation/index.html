How to Prevent Mold Remediation Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier
  • Aim for 30 to 50% indoor humidity and never let it stay above 60%, running dehumidifiers and ventilating as needed
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Address condensation on windows, pipes and cold surfaces promptly
  • Regular filter changes and HVAC maintenance help keep mold out of the ducts
  • Do not leave damp laundry, bath mats or spilled water sitting for long

When to Call for Mold Remediation in East Lansing

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Black, green, white or other colored growth you can see on surfaces in the building
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • Furniture against outside walls or items in closets developing speckled patches
  • An earthy, musty smell that will not go away, mostly in humid or closed-off spaces
  • Headaches, allergy flare-ups or breathing symptoms that ease whenever you leave the house
  • Spots you wipe away that keep reappearing in the same place
  • Windows, cold surfaces or walls that sweat with condensation on a regular basis

Why Hire a Professional for Mold Remediation?

This is not a job to take on yourself. When a colony is disturbed and nothing contains it, millions of spores go into the air, where they may settle in clean rooms and expose your family more than the mold did before. Professional mold remediation uses negative air pressure containment, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers and personal protective equipment so spores are captured and contained during removal.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Finding concealed moisture, drying it properly and preventing a repeat all take equipment and training, which certified remediation technicians have.
