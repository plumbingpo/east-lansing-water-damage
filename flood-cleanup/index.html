Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, East Lansing Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout East Lansing to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

After extraction and sanitizing, we set up commercial drying equipment, including LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, to dry the structure of your property thoroughly. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Health is not the only issue: when cleanup is done wrong, moisture stays hidden and heavy mold can follow in a matter of days. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Flood Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Add a sump pump with battery backup, since power outages and storms tend to arrive together
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Heavy rainfall, a dead sump pump and water spreading across the basement
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Dirty water rising out of floor drains or sewer lines into rooms you use
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
